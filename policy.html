Privacy policy.

How Moneyfinz collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: July 2026

1. Introduction

This policy explains what information Moneyfinz collects when you use our app and website, why we collect it, and the choices you have. By using Moneyfinz, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly, and a limited amount of information generated by using the app:

  • Account details — name, email address, and home currency.
  • Financial entries you add — transactions, categories, and budgets you create inside the app.
  • Basic device and usage data, such as app version and general performance metrics.

3. How we use information

Information is used to operate the app's core features — showing your balance, categorizing spending, tracking budgets — and to maintain and improve the service. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

4. Data security

Account and financial data are stored using industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest. Access to production systems is limited to authorized team members who need it to operate the service.

5. Your rights

You can review, correct, export, or delete your data at any time from within the app. If you'd rather we handle a request directly, contact us and we'll assist.

6. Cookies

Our website uses minimal local storage to remember basic preferences, such as a custom logo set on this site. It does not track you across other websites.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected with a new "last updated" date at the top of this page.

8. Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent to moneyfinz.contact@gmail.com or through our contact page.
